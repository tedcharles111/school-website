About Us

A Legacy of Learning

Founded in 1923, Latin College has been a beacon of academic excellence and character development for over a century. Our mission is to cultivate wisdom, virtue, and leadership in every student.

We offer a rigorous liberal arts curriculum grounded in the classics, fostering critical thinking, creativity, and ethical responsibility.
